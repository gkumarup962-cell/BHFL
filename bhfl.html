How it works

For customers

  1. Choose your financing requirement.
  2. Share basic information.
  3. Complete a preliminary assessment.
  4. Submit requested documents securely.
  5. Follow your application journey.
  6. Receive updates from the authorised team.

For partners

  1. Register your partner profile.
  2. Complete verification and onboarding.
  3. Log an eligible customer lead.
  4. Upload required documents securely.
  5. Track the case.
  6. View eligible business and payout records.
